Our Story

Where herbs meet jewels

A small brand with a simple belief: everyday care should be pure, gentle and beautiful.

Rooted in tradition

Gulora was born from the herbal remedies we grew up with: amla for strength, reetha for a gentle lather, shikakai for shine. We wanted to bring those time-honoured ingredients into products that feel as special as they are effective.

The name brings together what we love: the purity of herbs and the care you'd give to a treasured jewel. Every product we make is a small ritual of self-care, made by hand and made with heart.
